L01 Baseline Ladders

Dispatch→combine (layer0+layer1) latency for seven MoE baselines on the scenario-1 canon data basis: Kimi-K2 and Qwen3-235B routing, decode-slot window [64,96) with the previous window as every placement arm's oracle (g=0), token budgets b1–b64, warmup 5 + 10 timed iterations per cell.

How to read these numbers

e2e is the dispatch→combine span of the fused layer0+layer1 pass (CUDA events, clean path, no instrumentation); l0 / act / l1 are the critical rank's mutually exclusive sub-spans (the rank with max e2e each iteration), so l0+act+l1 = e2e by construction. plan is the per-iteration timed planning lane (SCHEMA rule 5); total = plan_comm + plan + e2e and is the number comparable to the handoff-13 settled tables. Every quoted value is the median of 10 iterations of the per-iteration max across ranks (per-metric for e2e/plan/total). All cells ran with FLUX_TEST_DETERMINISTIC=0, correctness skipped (perf campaign; arms were correctness-validated in the settled-table and flip capsules).

Standing caveats: wire-ordering (SCHEMA rule 5) probe status: every arm's dispatch AND combine leg carries a passing payload-probe verdict (handoff 08 rounds 1–5, incl. the epic-runner combine seam fix and the fused stage-2 lane-order fix) EXCEPT MoonEP's staged l01 combine, whose standalone probe host-OOMed and has no recorded verdict (its All2AllSingle primitive passed in the dispatch direction), and the staged EPLB combine direction, which has no leg-specific verdict of its own (same primitive class). Torch's NCCL collectives are out of rule-5 scope. Baselines were compared across capsules from ONE frozen binary (libflux_cuda.so 31f8446e…); all arms consumed byte-identical eval matrices, routing, and oracle sidecars per cell. These eval batches resample the canon windows — comm numbers are not byte-comparable to the M4 comm-ladder capsules (pool-sampled, no slot window).
