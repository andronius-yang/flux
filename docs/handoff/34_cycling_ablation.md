## 0. The ask and the verdict

The ablation figure must separate six rungs and show

```
COMET  >  {1 token-comm overlap,  2 placement only}  >  1+2 (no expert movement)
       >  1+2 + sequential expert swap  >  1+2 + OVERLAPPED expert swap   (best)
```

Verdict (details §4–§5):

1. **Rungs COMET > {1, 2} > 1+2 are delivered by the seen-basis data**
   (scenario S-A, every one of the 8 topics, and the main figure's
   livecodebench column). The new placement-only arm `pr0` beats COMET by
   3–4 ms on every seen topic.
2. **Rungs 1+2 > seq-swap > ovl-swap are delivered only when the eval topic
   is far from the placement basis** (professional_law under a basis that
   never saw it: reference max-pair load 2.5–3.1x its matched value). There
   the overlapped swap beats static 1+2 by 8–16 ms and the sequential swap
   by 1–9 ms. On every other topic the static placement is already at the
   floor and the swaps cost 0.5–6 ms.
3. **The postdoc's per-iteration topic schedule (phase-2 harness, placement
   carried over between topics) turns the two regimes into one mean.** With
   one severe topic in eight (scenario S-C, dwell 4) all rungs order as
   asked except "COMET > 2": the placement-only arm pays 80 ms on the severe
   block and lands 2 ms above COMET over the schedule. Increasing the
   severe share (S-B, one in four) makes the swap rungs wider and pushes
   *both* static placement arms to COMET or above; decreasing it (S-A,
   none) removes the swap rungs.
4. **Recommended figure data** (§5): a two-panel ablation — panel A =
   matched/seen basis for COMET > {1,2} > 1+2 (S-A per-topic means, or the
   main-figure column), panel B = the S-C dwell-4 schedule for
   1+2 > seq-swap > ovl-swap, with the per-block trajectory as the inset.
   A single-panel version exists only if rung "COMET > 2" is allowed to be
   a tie: S-C dwell 4 with medians (COMET 56.0, pr0 56.8).

## 3. Protocols

**Phase 1 — reset proxy (per-topic cells, `ablcycle_*` specs).** One topic
per cell (homog eval window [64,96)), oracle basis from `opool`. Drift is
emulated by restoring the oracle placement (tables + slot weights) before
timed iteration 0, 4, 8, 12 (`--swap_reset every --swap_reset_period 4`,
"dwell 4") or before every timed iteration ("dwell 1"). 16 timed + 5 warmup
iterations. Mean over the 16 iterations = 4 events + 12 quiet (dwell 4) or
16 events (dwell 1). New driver knob `--swap_reset_period` (default 1 =
previous behaviour).

**Phase 2 — topic schedule, placement carried over (`ablsched_*` specs).**
The postdoc's design, built 9/2 as an additive harness (python only):
family params `sched=poolA+poolB+…;dwell=N`; the runner generates one
sibling routing file per scheduled topic (the same files the per-topic
cells use), sizes every FLUX_A2AV_* knob and the NVSHMEM heap as the MAX
over the topics, and passes `--routing_sched_files/--routing_dwell` to the
flux (l01) and OURS drivers. Iteration i runs topic
`((i - last) // dwell) mod N` so the last timed iteration is topic 0 (the
correctness reference); the own-rank routing shard (and, for the flux
driver, the per-topic l0 output buffer and l1 row-scale) is switched
OUTSIDE the timed window; placement, swap tables and slot weights carry
over — no reset. OURS sizing folds the resident placement's reference route
and swap orbit on every scheduled topic. 16 timed iterations (dwell 1, two
or four cycles) or 32 (dwell 4, one cycle of 8 topics).

**Scenarios** (oracle basis → eval topics):

| | basis (previous window [32,64)) | evaluated topics | severe-topic share |
|---|---|---|---|
| S-A seen-8 | equal mix of all 8 pools | all 8 | 0 (max mismatch 1.8x on proLaw) |
| S-B unseen-4 | lcb + clinical + colmath + elecEng | hsPsych, hsWorldHist, philosophy, **proLaw** | 1/4 |
| S-C LOO-proLaw | 7 pools, proLaw excluded | all 8 | 1/8 |

Basis mismatch metric = the driver's `[sched-sizing]` reference-route
max-pair load on the resident placement (rows; proLaw matched = 6510):

| topic | S-A | S-B | S-C |
|---|---|---|---|
| professional_law | 11461 | 16391 | 20198 |
| every other topic | 5100–6900 | 5100–5400 | 4300–7300 |

## 5. Analysis and recommendation

1. **Two mechanisms, two regimes.** Placement (rung 2) removes the
   imbalance a correct basis can predict; the swap (rungs 5–6) removes the
   imbalance the basis got wrong. On a topic the basis has seen the swap
   has nothing to fix and costs its movement; on a topic the basis never
   saw the static placement is 20–25 ms above the floor and the swap
   recovers 10–16 ms of it, more when overlapped. The per-topic mismatch
   metric (§3) predicts which regime a topic is in: below ~1.5x matched,
   placement wins; above ~2x, the swap wins.
2. **The schedule mean is a mixture of the two regimes weighted by the
   severe share**, and the two static placement arms and the swap arms
   move in opposite directions with that share. 1/8 (S-C) is the point
   where the swap rungs are ordered with 0.5–2.5 ms margins while static
   1+2 still beats 1 and COMET; pr0 overshoots COMET by 2 ms (mean) or
   trails it by 0.8 ms (median). 1/4 (S-B) widens the swap rungs to 1–3 ms
   but sinks both static placement arms to COMET level.
3. **Overlap benefit scales with movement.** seq − ovl: full orbit 3.0 ms
   (S-A d1), 2.3 (S-B d1), 1.2 (S-B d4), 0.3 (S-C d4, movement mostly
   absorbed by dwell 4); one round ≤0.9 ms. Sequential full orbit at dwell
   1 is the worst swap arm everywhere (it exposes 4–5 slots/rank every
   iteration).
4. **Recommended data for the figure.**
   - Panel A (matched/seen basis; rungs COMET > 2 > 1 > 1+2): S-A phase-1
     means over the 8 topics (COMET 60.7, pr0 56.5, slip 54.4, static
     50.7), one binary, one allocation. The main-figure livecodebench
     column shows the same ordering (63.7 / llc 59.9 / 54.6 / 46.7) but
     across four binaries and with the EPIC-driver llc as rung 2.
   - Panel B (drift; rungs 1+2 > seq > ovl): S-C dwell-4 schedule medians
     (static 51.1, full-orbit seq 51.4 / ovl 51.2, one-round seq 51.9 /
     ovl 51.4) with the proLaw-block means as the annotated event
     (72.3 / 61.1 / 59.3 / 57.3 / 56.0) — or S-B dwell 4 if a wider swap
     margin matters more than the static arms' standing vs COMET.
   - If one panel is mandatory: S-C dwell 4, medians, with rung "COMET >
     2" reported as a tie (56.0 vs 56.8) and the proLaw block as the
     inset. Do not use S-B for a single panel (pr0 and static 1+2 sit at
     or above COMET).
   - Quote the mean over ≥3 same-allocation reps for any margin under
     2 ms (§4.4); cross-allocation offsets of ~1.5 ms were seen again
     today (COMET S-B per-topic 59.9 vs S-B schedule 56.7).
5. **Suggested ablation analysis for the paper text.** (a) the per-topic
   scatter: swap gain (static − best swap) vs basis mismatch, all 8+4+8
   topic cells — a clean monotone relation with the knee near 1.5x;
   (b) the S-C d4 per-iteration trajectory through the severe block
   (adaptation within 3 iterations, re-adaptation cost on exit); (c) the
   seq − ovl overlap benefit vs slots moved per iteration (§5.3).

## 6. Caveats and open items

- Single rep for most cells; reps only on the S-B schedule specs and the
  S-B per-topic spec (§4.4). Cross-capsule comparisons here are all
  same-binary but span two allocations (57861324: gate, S-A, S-B per-topic,
  S-B d4 OURS, S-A d1 OURS; 57867155: everything else).
- Stalled iterations (~90 ms, 1 in 32) appear on the static arm's
  college_math block in 2 of 3 schedule runs and once on a swap arm. Not
  root-caused; medians are reported alongside means.
- Phase-2 harness limits: the last timed iteration is always topic 0 (so
  the driver's final correctness check stays valid); the flux driver keeps
  one l0 output buffer per topic (8 × ~160 MB); the OURS sizing envelope
  is the max over topics of the resident placement's reference route plus
  each topic's swap orbit from the resident placement — chained
  placements beyond that envelope would show up as the recv-overflow
  assert (none seen). `route_global` arms are excluded.
- Two harness bugs found and fixed during the run (recorded in §7): s1
  demand sizing clamped to topic-0 demand (recv overflow spin), and the
  runner not passing the schedule flags to the flux driver (its first two
  schedule capsules' COMET/slipstream cells ran topic 0 only and are
  excluded; the recollect capsules replace them).
- A killed spinning step left allocation 57861324 in "nodes busy /
  step completing" for its last 1.6 h; the cure is `scancel` of the whole
  job, never of the step.
- Qwen is not covered; at Qwen 4n b64 the placement-only arm loses to
  COMET even when matched (55.5 vs 54.9 in the main figure).
- The main-figure label "1+2 + expert dispatch (re-solve every iteration,
  no expert movement)" describes an arm that moves experts.
