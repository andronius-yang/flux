02What the synthesis shows

OURS-s1 is the canonical winner from 8n up. It beats both of its own constituents everywhere they win separately — placement+routing and the slipstream combine compose. Qwen: −22% vs COMET at 4n b8, −40% at 8n b8, −49% at 16n b8.
16n b1/b2 belongs to the direct wire. Our plan lane over EPLB's staged All2AllSingle transport wins b1 by −25% (K2) / −18% (Qwen) against EPLB itself, and beats every fused arm — the handoff-29 registered prediction confirmed almost exactly (predicted 7.5–8.5, measured 7.22–7.49). Fused reclaims from b4.
s2 = a ~1–1.6 ms insurance premium. Always-solve + forced swap every iteration matches s1's e2e (movement fully overlapped) and pays only solve + host apply/issue on total. On this static eval distribution it buys nothing; at 16n K2 the forced swap develops an intermittent stall tail (e2e max 120 ms, median clean).
Placement-only never beats comm-only at 4n/8n. Its ~2.1–2.5 ms plan bracket outweighs transport gains at low hop counts; the two draw level at 16n b8+. Placement value scales with hops, comm value with bytes.
