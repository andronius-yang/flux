## Why not one scenario, and why not S-B

Placement (rung 2) removes the imbalance a basis can predict; the swap
(rungs 5–6) removes the imbalance the basis got wrong. On a seen basis the
combined arm is at the floor and the swap has nothing to recover (S-A: all
swap arms 0.4–1.4 ms above static). On an out-of-basis topic the static
placement is 20–25 ms above the floor and the swap recovers 12–16 ms of it,
more when overlapped (S-B/S-C professional_law). A schedule mean is these
two regimes weighted by the out-of-basis share, and the two rung groups move
in opposite directions with that share:

| share (scenario) | COMET > 2 | 1 > 1+2 | 1+2 > swap seq > ovl | ovl swap best |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (S-A, dwell 4) | +0.3 tie | +3.0 | absent | no (static best) |
| 1/8 (S-C, dwell 4) | −1.4 / median −0.3 | +1.2 | +0.7 / +0.7 | yes, +2.6 over 1 |
| 1/4 (S-B, dwell 4) | −4.2 | −2.2 | +1.7 / +0.7 | tie with 1 (+0.2) |

S-B (basis from 4 pools, cycle over the 4 unseen topics) widens the swap
rungs but sinks both placement arms to COMET level and lets the
drift-insensitive token-comm arm tie the best swap arm. Below 1/8 the
placement-only rung flips back but the swap rungs fall under what 3 reps
resolve (0.5 ms). Hence the split: panel A shows what the optimizations do
when the placement basis is right, panel B what the expert movement adds
when it is wrong.

Mechanism metric (driver `[sched-sizing]` reference-route max-pair load,
rows; professional_law matched = 6510): professional_law 11.5k (S-A) /
16.4k (S-B) / 20.2k (S-C); every other topic 4.3k–7.3k. Swap gain tracks
this number (`mismatch_vs_gain.csv`).

## Caveats to carry into the caption

- Panel A is single-rep per topic (8 topics act as the replicate); panel B
  has 3 same-allocation reps (rep sd 0.1–1.0 ms). Compare arms only inside
  a panel: COMET moved 4–6 ms between allocations on three topics.
- Panel B by median is a tie between static and the swap arms (the median
  ignores the 4 event iterations of 32); the means and the block are the
  honest statistics for a drift figure, and the inset shows why.
- The static arm shows one ~40 ms stalled iteration per run on the
  college_math block under dwell 1 (every rep) and in some dwell-4 runs;
  not root-caused; medians are unaffected.
- The expert exchange is already fully hidden from the GEMM (nsys, handoff
  34 §5b); the seq-vs-ovl rung measures the copy + wait (~7 ms per event,
  0.7 ms on the schedule mean), and the overlapped arm's remaining cost is
  host decision time (~2 ms per event) plus the inter-node imbalance an
  intra-node swap cannot reach (~8 ms on the block).
