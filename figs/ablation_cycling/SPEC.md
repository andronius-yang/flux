# Ablation figure — specification (REV 0, 2026-09-03)

One single-column bar chart, two groups (S-A, S-C), two versions from one
parameterized script (`make_figure.py`, every **[knob]** below lives in its
KNOBS block). Data: `figure_src.csv` (generated by `make_figure_src.py`;
provenance in `DECISION.md`). Style inherits the main-figure and
weak-scaling rulings (palette, fonts, edge stroke, hatch channel).

## 1. Size and placement

- Single-column NSDI figure: width = `\columnwidth` = **3.33 in**
  **[knob: FIG_W]**. Figure + caption ≤ **1/4 of the 9 in text height**
  (2.25 in) → figure PDF **1.90 in** tall **[knob: FIG_H]**, leaving
  ~0.35 in for a caption. (Weak-scaling used 1/5 → 1.45 in; this one gets
  the extra height because the vertical speedup labels need it.)
- Margins **[knob: MARGINS]**: left 0.135, right 0.995, bottom 0.17,
  top 0.80 of the figure (legend lives in the top 0.20).
- Exported at final physical size; fonts embedded (`pdf.fonttype 42`).

## 2. What is drawn

One axes, y = total latency (ms) **[knob: Y_LABEL]** = plan_comm + plan +
e2e, per-iteration max across ranks, mean over the timed iterations, then
mean over ALL reps. Two bar groups on the x axis, no x title:

| group | scenario (short label under the group **[knob: GROUP_LABELS]**) | data rows in `figure_src.csv` |
|---|---|---|
| left | **S-A** — seen basis, professional_law (4 reps) | `panel = A: seen basis (S-A per-topic)`, `topic = proLaw`, statistic `mean over 16 timed iters` |
| right | **S-C** — drift schedule, 1 in 8 topics unseen (3 reps) | `panel = B: drift schedule (S-C dwell 4, ...)`, `topic = ALL`, statistic `whole-schedule mean, mean over reps` |

Each group holds the same **chain of 4 bars, baseline left → best
right**, adjacent (no gap inside a group). The chain differs between the
two versions **[knob: VERSION]**:

| bar | verA (comm first) | verB (placement first) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | COMET | COMET |
| 2 | w/ token-dispatch computation overlap | w/ expert placement & routing |
| 3 | + expert placement & routing | + token-dispatch computation overlap |
| 4 | + expert-dispatch overlap (composed expert swap, overlapped) | same |

Bar 3 is the same arm in both versions (`1+2 (no expert movement)`); bar
4 is the full-orbit overlapped swap (`1+2 + expert swap, overlapped`) —
the composed multi-slot exchange, chosen over the one-round policy because
it is the "expert dispatch" mechanism and holds the chain in both groups.
**[knob: SWAP_ARM]** switches to the one-round arm. **[knob: INCLUDE_SEQ]**
(default off) inserts the sequential-swap twin before bar 4 as a 5th bar
("+ expert dispatch, un-overlapped") for a version that shows the overlap
rung explicitly.

Numbers the bars carry (ms; speedup = COMET / arm):

| bar | verA S-A | verA S-C | verB S-A | verB S-C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| COMET | 65.84 (1.00×) | 57.61 (1.00×) | 65.84 (1.00×) | 57.61 (1.00×) |
| bar 2 | 58.61 (1.12×) | 54.77 (1.05×) | 61.27 (1.07×) | 59.01 (**0.98×**) |
| bar 3 | 54.32 (1.21×) | 53.56 (1.08×) | 54.32 (1.21×) | 53.56 (1.08×) |
| bar 4 | 52.86 (1.25×) | 52.24 (1.10×) | 52.86 (1.25×) | 52.24 (1.10×) |

verB's S-C bar 2 sits *above* COMET (placement without comm overlap is the
arm most exposed to the out-of-basis block; DECISION.md). The script draws
it as measured and prints its label as `0.98×`; do not hide it. **[knob:
FLAG_BELOW_ONE]** (default on) sets that label in the secondary ink so it
reads as a reported miss rather than a typo.

## 3. Bar styling

- Width: bars fill their slot (slot = 1 unit, bar width 1.0
  **[knob: BAR_WIDTH]**), so bars in a group touch. Group gap
  **[knob: GROUP_GAP]** = 0.7 slot. Padding between the first/last bar and
  the axes frame **[knob: EDGE_PAD]** = 0.45 slot. With 4 bars per group:
  x extent = 0.45 + 4 + 0.7 + 4 + 0.45 = 9.6 slots.
- Fill = the feature-stack encoding **[knob: STACK_STYLE]**, cumulative
  along the chain so that "one more feature" reads as "one step darker":
  COMET olive `#999933`, no hatch (the same hue COMET has in every other
  figure); Ours bars in steel-blue tints of `#4878b0`: bar 2 = 40 % tint
  with hatch `///`, bar 3 = 70 % tint with hatch `\\\`, bar 4 = full
  `#4878b0` with hatch `xx`. Hatch linewidth 0.4 pt in the edge ink; edge
  stroke 0.45 pt primary ink `#0b0b0b` on every bar (print/grayscale
  robustness, as in the main figure). The tints are the same in both
  versions; only the legend text for bars 2 and 3 changes.
- Speedup labels **[knob: SPEEDUP_LABELS]**: `"{:.2f}×"` set **vertical**
  (rotation 90), centered on the bar, baseline 0.25 ms **[knob:
  LABEL_PAD]** above the bar top, 5.8 pt, primary ink; COMET's label reads
  `1.00×` **[knob: COMET_LABEL]**. Ours bars' labels are not bold (the main
  figure bolds only the headline system; here every Ours bar is the
  system).
- Y limits **[knob: Y_LIM]**: bottom = 0 by default **[knob: Y_ZERO]**;
  the alternative `Y_ZERO=False` starts at 40 ms with a broken-axis glyph
  (rejected by default — a truncated axis exaggerates 1.05× gaps). Top =
  ceil_nice(max bar × 1.30) **[knob: HEADROOM]** so the tallest vertical
  label (~5 characters at 5.8 pt ≈ 0.22 in ≈ 12 ms of axis at this height)
  never touches the legend.
- COMET reference **[knob: COMET_REF]** (default on): a hairline
  (0.4 pt, `#898781`, dashed 2/1.5) across each group at that group's COMET
  height so the "vs COMET" reading is visible even where an Ours bar is
  taller (verB S-C).
- Grid: horizontal hairlines only, 0.4 pt `#e1e0d9`, behind bars; no
  vertical grid; axis line `#c3c2b7`; ticks outward, 6 pt.

## 4. Text

- Group labels **[knob: GROUP_LABELS]** under each group, two lines,
  6 pt, primary ink:
  `S-A: seen basis\n(prof. law, 4 reps)` and
  `S-C: drift schedule\n(1/8 unseen, 3 reps)`.
  Alternative one-liners `seen basis` / `1-in-8 drift` **[knob]**.
- Y label `Total latency (ms)` 6.5 pt. No x title.
- Legend **[knob: LEGEND]** on top, outside the axes, flush to the top
  edge, one row of 4 swatches (fill+hatch+edge), 6 pt, no frame, column
  spacing 0.8; falls to 2+2 if it oversets 3.33 in at final size. Texts
  **[knob: LEGEND_TEXTS]**: verA `COMET`, `w/ token-dispatch comp. overlap`,
  `+ expert placement & routing`, `+ expert-dispatch overlap`; verB swaps
  the middle two. The `+` prefix encodes the cumulative stack.
- Font **[knob: FONT_FAMILY]**: `["Helvetica", "Arial", "DejaVu Sans"]`;
  sizes **[knob: FONT_SIZES]**: legend 6, y label 6.5, ticks 6, group
  labels 6, bar labels 5.8.

## 5. Data plumbing

`make_figure.py` reads `figure_src.csv` only (no capsule access), selects
the rows named in §2 by (panel prefix, topic, statistic, tidy_arm), and
refuses to run if any of the 8 needed rows is missing or has `n` below
**[knob: MIN_REPS]** (default 3). Arm keys **[knob: ARM_ROWS]**:

| bar | `tidy_arm` (S-A rows carry the `d4` suffix, S-C rows do not) |
|---|---|
| COMET | `COMET` |
| token-comm overlap | `1 token-comm overlap` |
| placement & routing | `2 placement only (pr0)` |
| both | `1+2 static` |
| + swap ovl | `1+2 full-orbit swap OVL d4` / `1+2 full-orbit swap OVL` |
| (+ swap seq, optional) | `1+2 full-orbit swap SEQ d4` / `1+2 full-orbit swap SEQ` |

Speedups are recomputed in the script from the means (never typed in).
Outputs `ablation_verA.{pdf,png}`, `ablation_verB.{pdf,png}` (PNG 300 dpi
preview), deterministic PDFs (fixed metadata, `pdf.fonttype 42`).

## 6. Render checklist

1. PDF height 1.90 in, width 3.33 in; `pdffonts` shows no Type 3.
2. No speedup label touches the legend or another label; the `0.98×`
   label in verB S-C sits above its (taller) bar with the reference
   hairline visible below it.
3. Bars inside a group touch; the two groups are separated by a visible
   gap narrower than one bar.
4. Legend reads left-to-right in the same order as the bars.
5. Grayscale print: the four fills remain distinguishable by hatch alone.

## 7. Caption skeleton (for the paper, not drawn)

"Ablation at K2, 4 nodes, 64 MiB per rank. Bars accumulate optimizations
left to right; labels give the speedup over COMET. S-A: placement solved
on the previous window of the 8-topic mix, traffic from professional_law
(the mix's hardest topic), 4 reps. S-C: the same 8 topics cycled 4
iterations each with the placement carried over and professional_law
excluded from the placement basis, 3 reps. Placement-only exceeds COMET on
S-C (0.98×) because it has no communication overlap to absorb the
out-of-basis block; combined with the overlap it leads."
